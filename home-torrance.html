Expert Hand Therapy Near Torrance

Personalized to Help You Rebuild, Restore, and Reach Beyond.

Expert Hand Therapy — Just Minutes from Torrance

Reach Beyond Therapy is located in nearby Redondo Beach, approximately 5–10 minutes from most Torrance neighborhoods via Artesia Blvd or PCH. Our clinic is led by Certified Hand Therapist Jade Wong, who brings over a decade of upper extremity expertise and sees each patient one-on-one — no shared sessions, no aide hand-offs.

Active in Torrance?

Here's Who We Help

Torrance has a deeply active community — from golfers at the local courses to cyclists on Crenshaw, baseball and softball players in recreational leagues, and office workers dealing with repetitive strain from long hours at a desk.

We also treat caregivers managing overuse injuries, post-operative patients recovering from hand or elbow surgeries, and anyone whose hands or arms are holding them back from daily life.

Our Approach

Boutique Care. Real Results.

We accept a limited patient load so every session gets the attention it deserves. You'll always work directly with Jade — a Certified Hand Therapist with rigorous national credentials and over 10 years of clinical experience in upper extremity rehabilitation.

Your treatment plan is built around your specific injury, your goals, and your lifestyle — not a template.
