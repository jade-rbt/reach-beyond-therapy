Searching for a “Certified Hand Therapist”?

What Is a CHT — and Why Does It Matter?

A Certified Hand Therapist (CHT) is an occupational or physical therapist who has earned an advanced credential from the Hand Therapy Certification Commission (HTCC). To sit for the exam, a therapist needs at least three years of clinical practice and a minimum of 4,000 hours of direct hand therapy experience — then has to pass a comprehensive certification exam covering the anatomy, conditions, surgeries, and rehab of the entire upper extremity.

Fewer than 7,000 therapists in the U.S. hold the credential. For you, that means the person treating your trigger finger, carpal tunnel, or post-op repair has spent years doing exactly that — not treating hands occasionally between knees and backs.

Curious how this differs from general physical therapy? Read Hand Therapy vs. Physical Therapy.

Common Reasons South Bay Patients Come In

Conditions We Treat for Torrance Patients

Trigger Finger

Golfers, gardeners, and anyone gripping a mouse, steering wheel, or tools all day — from the Del Amo offices to Torrance’s business parks — know that catching, clicking finger. Conservative care with a custom resting splint often helps.

Trigger finger treatment

Carpal Tunnel Syndrome

Numb or tingling fingers at night, dropping things, aching after long days at the keyboard or on the production floor. We address splinting, nerve glides, and the daily habits that keep it flaring.

Carpal tunnel therapy

Custom Splinting

Thumb spica, wrist cock-up, mallet finger, relative motion orthoses and more — molded to your hand on-site instead of an off-the-shelf brace that never quite fits.

Custom splinting

Post-Surgical Rehab

After a tendon repair, fracture fixation, or release surgery, we follow your surgeon’s protocol closely to protect the repair while restoring motion and strength.

Post-op hand rehab

Tennis & Golfer’s Elbow

Elbow pain from swinging a club, a racket, or a hammer — or from repetitive lifting at work. We focus on tendon loading and getting you back to the activity, not just resting it.

Elbow therapy

Hand & Wrist Fractures

From a fall on an outstretched hand to a jammed finger on the court, we protect healing bones with custom splints, then rebuild motion and grip.

Fracture rehab
