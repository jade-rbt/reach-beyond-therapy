Expert One-on-One Care Right Here in Redondo Beach

At Reach Beyond Therapy, you won't be handed off to an aide or shuffled through a group session. Every visit is one-on-one with Jade Wong, a Certified Hand Therapist with over a decade of clinical expertise — focused entirely on your hand, wrist, elbow, or shoulder recovery.

Our Community

Built for Redondo Beach's Active Lifestyle

Redondo Beach isn't a sit-still kind of place. Our patients are kayakers and paddleboarders launching from the pier, volleyball players at Seaside Lagoon, cyclists on the strand, and pickleball regulars who refuse to miss a match.

We also treat working professionals, post-surgical patients, musicians, parents, and anyone whose hands or arms are keeping them from what they love most.

What Brings Redondo Patients In

Hand, Wrist & Elbow Care in Redondo Beach

Carpal Tunnel Syndrome

Numb, tingling fingers at night or after a paddle session or long ride on the strand. Splinting, nerve glides, and setup changes — often before surgery is ever on the table.

Carpal tunnel therapy

Trigger Finger

A finger that clicks, catches, or locks after gripping a paddle, a pickleball paddle, or garden tools. Custom resting splints and tendon gliding.

Trigger finger treatment

Custom Splinting

Thermoplastic splints molded to your hand on-site — same visit when medically necessary — and adjusted as you heal.

Custom splinting

Post-Surgical Rehab

Tendon repairs, fracture fixation, and releases — we follow your surgeon’s protocol closely to protect the repair and rebuild function.

Post-op hand rehab

We treat much more — see every condition on our services page.

Your Redondo Beach Hand Therapist

Jade Wong, OTR/L, CHT

Jade founded Reach Beyond Therapy right here in Redondo Beach. She’s a licensed occupational therapist and Certified Hand Therapist with over a decade in upper extremity rehab, Treasurer of the Hand Therapy Society of Greater Los Angeles, and a member of the American Society of Hand Therapists.

She’s also a climber and triathlete, which is why her plans aim at getting you back to your activity — not just out of pain.
